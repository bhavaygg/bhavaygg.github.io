Bhavay Aggarwal

I am a final-year Bioinformatics PhD student at Georgia Institute of Technology, working in the Sinha Lab.

My work focuses on computational methods for spatial transcriptomics and single-cell omics. I build tools to study cellular organization, subcellular transcript localization, and cell-cell communication, with a broader interest in separating biological signal from stochastic variation.

Before Georgia Tech, I completed my Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science and Biosciences at IIIT Delhi.
